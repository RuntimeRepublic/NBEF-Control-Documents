## 4. The Regulative Principles of Liberty, as tests for education rules

Each principle is stated as a **test question** an NBEF document must answer. The answers belong to later CONTROLs.

| # | Principle | KJB | Test question for an NBEF document |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP1 | **God makes the rules.** Rulers are ministers, not owners. | Romans 13:4 | What written authority does this education rule trace to, and whose authority is it? |
| RP2 | **What is authorized is permitted; what is not authorized is forbidden to government.** | Deuteronomy 4:2 (Analogy) | Where is this requirement granted in writing? If nowhere, it is not required. |
| RP3 | **No elite capture.** | Genesis 3:3; Matthew 15:9 | Has any official, board, or majority added a binding requirement beyond the written standard? |
| RP4 | **Rain on the just and the unjust.** | Matthew 5:45; Deuteronomy 1:17 | Does the rule treat every family and every student the same way? |
| RP5 | **Enumerate, limit, protect.** | Exodus 20:13, 15; Leviticus 25:10 | Does it enumerate and limit the power it uses, and protect life, liberty, and property? |
| RP6 | **Remember.** | Psalm 78:5–7, 9–11 | Does it pass the works of God in history to the next generation, so they do not turn back in the day of battle? |

**RP6 is the education principle in Scripture's own words.** God "appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children: That the generation to come might know them, even the children which should be born; who should arise and declare them to their children: That they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments" (Psalm 78:5–7). The generation that forgot, "being armed, and carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle" (78:9). See also "And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children" (Deuteronomy 6:7).

## 6. Method for a Ruling

0. State the question being tested.
1. Read the whole story in the KJB, with what comes before and after.
2. Compare Scripture with Scripture.
3. Name every counter-text.
3a. If the English is disputed or carries the Ruling, consult the Original Texts. Record the word, its meaning, and the source.
4. Classify the link: Command, Principle, Precedent, Analogy, or Modern design.
4a. Apply the test questions RP1 to RP6 (Section 4).
5a. Call the witnesses: do they confirm it produces results?
5. State the Ruling in one sentence, with its date and version.
6. Record what would change the Ruling.

For AI assistants and the NBEF Control Steward: use the KJB only through step 4a. Go to step 3a and step 5a only when the Human Engineer asks or the question is a Ruling. Fail closed: if a step needs doctrine NBEF has not adopted, stop and return the question to the Human Engineer.
